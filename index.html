Yori Jomo

Yori Jomo is a Copenhagen-based footwear brand, producing in Italy with a focus on craftsmanship, quality materials, and timeless design.

Yori Jomo in japanease means ....

Founded with the idea of creating shoes that feel understated, refined, and easy to wear across seasons, Yori Jomo combines Scandinavian simplicity with traditional Italian shoemaking.

The collections are designed for women who appreciate well-made products rather than fast trends — styles intended to last both aesthetically and in quality.

Produced in small quantities and handcrafted in Tuscany, Italy, developed with carefully selected Italian suppliers, each style reflects a balance between comfort, functionality, and quiet elegance.
